S. Liu received the Ph.D. degree from Chengdu University of Technology, Chengdu, China, in 2024. From 2024 to 2025, he was a Visiting Ph.D. Student with the Faculty of ITC, University of Twente, Enschede, The Netherlands.

Since 2025, he has been a Lecturer with the School of Computer Engineering, Chengdu Technological University, Chengdu.

His research interests lie in machine learning and image processing.
